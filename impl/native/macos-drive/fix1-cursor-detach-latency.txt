  detached vs cursor-off residual:    0.02s  (detach captures ~all of the cursor cost)

The residual ~1.5s is the pixel-click background settle floor (focus-free safety), present
with the cursor OFF too, so it is not the cursor. AX element clicks take the no-cursor-move
path and never paid the glide. type_text and pixel/coordinate clicks did, and now do not.
